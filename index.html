Live Fish Purchase & Customer Policy

  1. Minimum Order: Minimum order quantity is 3 pairs of fish. Orders below the minimum quantity will not be accepted. Fish availability depends on current stock.
  2. Fish Selection & Availability: Varieties can be selected from available stock. Photos/videos are representative; exact colour, size, pattern and appearance may vary slightly. Availability will be confirmed before payment.
  3. Pre-Shipment Video: A packing/pre-shipment video can be provided. Customers should check it before shipment.
  4. Safe Packing & Shipping: Fish are packed using appropriate live-fish methods. Shipping is available where permitted and serviceable. Shipping charges are payable by the customer unless an offer says otherwise.
  5. Live Arrival / DOA: Customers must record a continuous unboxing video showing the unopened package, shipping label, opening, all fish and their condition immediately after opening. Edited or incomplete videos are not accepted for DOA claims. Report a dead-on-arrival fish as soon as possible, preferably within 2 hours, with clear photos and the complete video.
  6. Refund / Replacement: Refund or replacement is considered after verification for eligible DOA cases. Shipping charges are generally non-refundable unless otherwise agreed.
  7. No Refund After Customer Handling: Refunds normally do not apply to losses caused by unsuitable tanks, poor water quality, sudden temperature changes, overfeeding, disease/infection from other fish, or improper handling.
  8. Acclimatization: Float the bag approximately 15–20 minutes to equalize temperature, gradually introduce aquarium water, avoid untreated tap water, and observe new fish before mixing.
  9. Delivery Delays: Courier, weather, transportation, holidays, strikes, natural events and other courier issues may affect delivery time.
  10. Incorrect Address / Unavailability: Customers are responsible for correct name, address, PIN code and contact number. Additional charges/losses may apply for failed delivery caused by customer information or unavailability.
  11. Cancellation: Orders may be cancelled only before packing/dispatch, subject to farm confirmation. Cancellation may not be possible after packing or dispatch.
  12. Payment: Orders are processed after payment confirmation. Keep the payment receipt/screenshot. An order is confirmed only after payment and stock are verified.
  13. Fish Size & Natural Variation: Size, colour intensity, pattern, tail shape, gender development and growth can vary naturally. Minor differences from photos/videos are normal.
  14. Customer Responsibility: Maintain good water quality, suitable temperature, filtration/aeration, suitable tank size, suitable food, quarantine and acclimatization.
  15. IMPORTANT CLAIM RULE: 🎥 UNBOXING VIDEO IS MANDATORY FOR DOA CLAIMS. Do not discard packaging or fish before contacting the farm if there is a problem.
  16. Contact: Shastha Guppy Farm, Chikkamagaluru – 577101, Karnataka. Phone: 8088820799.
